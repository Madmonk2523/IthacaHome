# IthacaHome photography sources — V7

All photographs used on the site are served from local `/assets/` paths in the new repository. The 22 historic gallery photos were archived from the owner's original Wix site and are stored in `assets/property-gallery/` in GitHub. The additional community and interior photographs were supplied by the client or extracted from their marketing flyer.

The visitor-facing pages do not hotlink Wix or Apartments.com photos. The Apartments.com 3D tour link opens on their website and requires that external site to remain available.

Every rendered content-image path is assigned to exactly one place across the entire website. See `photo-manifest.json` and `PHOTO_PLACEMENT.md` for placements. Appearance-based checks can confirm exact local duplicates; visually similar variants in archived photos should be reviewed during final owner proofing.

Client should confirm rights to all property photography before publication.
