Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Information you provide

When you submit a leasing inquiry, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, preferred property, move-in timing and message.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to leasing inquiries, arrange property tours and communicate about the residences. We do not use inquiry form details for public profiles or listings.

Email processing

Forms may use a third-party email-delivery provider to route your message to the IthacaHome leasing team. Website hosting and basic security services may process technical request information.

External links

Our website may link to third-party services, including Google Maps and rental listings. Their privacy practices are separate from ours.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email rentals@ithacahome.com or call (607) 379-6001.
