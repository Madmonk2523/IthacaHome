06 / THE DETAILS

Good to know.

Answers to a few common questions, adapted from IthacaHome's existing rental website.

Still curious? Let's talk ↗
01Are leases typically one year?+
Yes. Leases are generally annual, although shorter stays are sometimes possible. Ask the leasing team about current options.
02Are the residences furnished?+
Residences are normally rented unfurnished. Photos may show staged spaces. Inquire if you need a furnished option.
03Are pets welcome?+
Yes—pets are welcome, subject to the community's current pet rules. The first pet has no additional monthly charge; ask about additional pets and any applicable restrictions.
04How far is Cornell?+
The existing IthacaHome website describes the Cornell main campus as approximately one mile away. A nearby TCAT stop provides another option; check published bus timetables for current service.
05What's included in the rent?+
The owner's leasing flyer lists internet, cable, water, sewer and snow removal among included services. Check the terms of your specific lease for the final inclusions and fees.
06How do I arrange a tour or ask about availability?+
Send a leasing inquiry online, or email rentals@ithacahome.com. The leasing team can share current availability, pricing and viewing options.
