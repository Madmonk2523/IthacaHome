LEASING QUESTIONS / 06

Good to know
before you go.

Quick answers to common leasing questions. Reach out anytime for details on a specific home.

Are the residences furnished?

Residences are unfurnished. If you have questions about a specific unit, the leasing team is happy to help.

How long is a typical lease?

Leases are generally for one year. Shorter terms may occasionally be possible; inquire directly.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Cats and dogs are welcome, up to three pets per home. The first pet is free and each additional pet is $25 per month. Pets over 50 lb carry an extra $25 per month. Pit bulls, Rottweilers and German Shepherds are not accepted. Contact the leasing team to confirm current pet terms or to ask about an accommodation.

What is included, and which utilities are separate?

Internet, snow removal and an in-unit washer/dryer come at no extra charge, and water and sewer are covered up to a quarterly cap. Gas, electric and solid-waste charges are billed separately. Ask the leasing team for the current details for your unit.

Are private bathrooms available?

Every Plaza East bedroom has an attached private bathroom. Some SummerHill layouts do as well. Ask the leasing team about a specific floor plan.

Is resident parking available?

Yes. Resident parking is free, and garages are available to rent. Contact the leasing team for current garage availability.

Is there transportation to Cornell?

East Hill Plaza and its TCAT stop are about a three-minute walk away. Cornell is about a 15-minute walk or three-minute drive. Routes 82 and 51 serve the area; check current TCAT schedules.

How do I arrange a tour?

Use the inquiry form or email rentals@ithacahome.com to coordinate an in-person visit. Virtual 3D tours are also available.
