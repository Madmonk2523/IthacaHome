LEASING QUESTIONS / 06

Good to know
before you go.

Answers to a few common leasing questions. The team can confirm current terms for a specific unit.

Are the residences furnished?

The supplied rental information lists the residences as unfurnished. Ask the leasing team whether any current exceptions apply.

How long is a typical lease?

Leases are generally for one year. Shorter terms may occasionally be possible; inquire directly.

Are pets welcome?

Cats and dogs are permitted subject to breed restrictions. The existing IthacaHome FAQ names pit bulls, Rottweilers and German shepherds among breeds not accepted, and sets a three-pet-per-unit maximum. It lists no added fee for the first pet and $25 per month for each additional pet. The supplied 2025?26 sheet also lists an extra $25 per month for pets over 50 pounds. Policies and fees can change; confirm the current rules with leasing. Contact the team about accommodation requests.

What is included, and which utilities are separate?

The supplied 2025?26 sheet lists internet, snow removal and in-unit washer/dryer at no extra charge, with water and sewer subject to a quarterly cap. Gas/electric usage and solid-waste charges are listed separately. These are historical terms, not a current quote; ask leasing to confirm today's inclusions and costs.

Are private bathrooms available?

Every Plaza East bedroom has an attached private bathroom. Some SummerHill layouts do as well; check with leasing about the specific floor plan.

Is resident parking available?

The supplied information lists free resident parking and optional garage rentals. Contact leasing for current garage availability and pricing.

Is there transportation to Cornell?

East Hill Plaza and its TCAT stop are about a three-minute walk away. Cornell is about a 15-minute walk or three-minute drive. Routes 82 and 51 serve the area; check current TCAT schedules.

How do I arrange a tour?

Use the inquiry form or email rentals@ithacahome.com to coordinate an in-person visit. Virtual 3D tours are also available.
