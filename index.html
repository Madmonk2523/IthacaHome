LEASING QUESTIONS / 06

Good to know
before you go.

Answers to a few common leasing questions. The team can confirm current terms for a specific unit.

Are the residences furnished?

The existing IthacaHome rental information lists the residences as unfurnished. Contact leasing about any exceptions.

How long is a typical lease?

Leases are generally for one year. Shorter terms may occasionally be possible; inquire directly.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Pet limits, breed restrictions and additional fees may apply. Confirm the current pet policy before applying.

Is there transportation to Cornell?

TCAT bus service is available near East Hill Plaza. Check current routes and schedules at TCAT before planning a trip.

How do I arrange a tour?

Use the inquiry form or email rentals@ithacahome.com to coordinate a visit.
