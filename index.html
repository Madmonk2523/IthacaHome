GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Questions about leasing? We’re happy to help.

What floor plans are available?+

Plaza East offers 2-, 3- and 4-bedroom layouts; SummerHill has 2- and 3-bedroom townhouses. Contact the leasing team for current availability.

Are utilities included?+

The community lists internet, cable, water, sewer, and snow removal as included. Confirm details for the specific unit you choose.

Are the residences furnished?+

Residences are generally offered unfurnished. Ask about options if furnishings are important to you.

Can I tour before applying?+

Absolutely. Send a tour request and the leasing team can help coordinate a visit. Virtual tours are also available on the property's Apartments.com listing .
